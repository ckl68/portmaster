FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Useful answers for first conversations.

If your move has special requirements, send the details through the quote form and we can review the shipment directly.

What is drayage?+

Drayage generally refers to short-distance container transportation between ports, rail ramps, warehouses, and nearby freight facilities.

What do you need to prepare a quote?+

The most useful information is the pickup location, delivery destination, container size, estimated timing, and any shipment-specific requirements.

Can you review both import and export moves?+

Yes. Availability and scope depend on the lane and the details of the shipment, but both import and export requests can be reviewed.

Do you support rail-related moves?+

Yes. If your shipment involves a rail ramp or intermodal handoff, include that information in your request so it can be assessed properly.
